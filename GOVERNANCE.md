# Governance

Matthew Chenoweth Wright / Monolithic LLC maintains this initial research repository.
No external board, independent evaluator or institutional sponsor is appointed here.

Propose changes through an issue or pull request. Explain the claim, source, affected
articles, operational effect, conflicts and tests. Preserve the original source.
Material normative changes require a versioned amendment and recorded rationale;
implementation changes require an updated policy version when behavior changes.

Written participation and asynchronous responses are first-class. No one must disclose
diagnoses, private beliefs or cognitive data to submit a critique. Identify conflicts
of interest; label author self-checks explicitly. Do not count likes, generated assent,
or informal discussion as adoption, endorsement, consent or peer review.

Maintain a decision log in release notes. For disputed implementation outcomes retain
the counterexample and mark the affected claim UNRESOLVED until examined. For a proposed
real-world pilot, agree who can stop it, how evidence is protected, who reviews disputes,
and what happens when permission is withdrawn before starting.
