# Contributing

Start with a bounded claim: what should happen, under which conditions, and what
observation would falsify it? Include a minimal reproducer, exact commit and toolchain,
expected/observed behavior and evidence limits. Synthetic data are preferred.

For philosophical or governance proposals, include the strongest counterargument and
relevant rights conflicts. For legal claims, name jurisdiction, date and primary sources;
do not treat the UDCR as enacted law. Keep empirical claims distinct from advocacy.

Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` and `python scripts/check_sources.py`.
Preserve original source bytes. Add regression tests for permission-policy changes.
Do not expose secrets, private correspondence or third-party personal information.
Do not send contributor material to other services without permission.

Licensing is pending as described in LICENSE-STATUS.md; obtain clarity before adding
third-party material or relying on redistribution rights.
