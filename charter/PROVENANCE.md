`python scripts/check_sources.py` checks the source blob, manifest, deterministic
transcription and relative documentation links. `--render` regenerates the Markdown
transcription after first checking the archived bytes. Whitespace and heading markup
are normalized for readability; wording, articles, subclauses and epilogue are retained.

The source is evidence of what was published, not evidence that all its claims are true
or legally effective. Its epilogue's inevitability language and equal-legal-standing
proposal are not findings established by this repository. Future revisions go in the
[amendment record](AMENDMENTS.md); never overwrite the original.
