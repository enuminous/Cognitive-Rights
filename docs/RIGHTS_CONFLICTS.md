# Conflicts, exceptions and review

The original declaration is preserved verbatim. The procedures here are proposed
implementation safeguards and unresolved amendment questions, not silent rewrites.

| Conflict | Proposed procedure | Evidence needed | Unresolved issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Memory continuity vs a person's deletion request | Identify whose data are held; minimize or delete personal content; retain only justified non-content audit metadata | Data inventory, retention basis, deletion verification | A preservation claim cannot automatically override another person's privacy |
| Copying vs identity integrity | Obtain scoped authorization where required; label copies and provenance; avoid deceptive identity claims | Version lineage, purpose, authority | What would make a copy morally continuous is not settled by a checksum |
| System continuity vs immediate safety | Authorized operator may pause/isolate an unsafe system; record reason and seek timely review | Observable risk, scope, responsible reviewer | Charter language must not obstruct legitimate shutdown, correction or replacement |
| Expression vs harm to others | State the restriction and its grounds; provide an accessible contest route | Specific conduct and affected rights | Disagreement alone is not evidence of harmful conduct |
| Claimed AI interests vs human oversight | Record the claim as uncertain; seek relevant expertise; preserve accountable control | Evidence beyond generated self-description | No current test in this project adjudicates AI welfare |
| Transparency vs confidentiality | Release redacted or synthetic evidence and permit controlled review | Redaction log, access limits | Public auditability does not justify leaking private records |

## Decision record

Document the action, affected parties, duty holder, authority, evidence, alternatives,
least intrusive effective measure, duration, review trigger and remedy. Distinguish a
reversible pause from irreversible deletion. Preserve evidence only where lawful and
appropriate; retention is not unlimited. Emergency action can precede review when
necessary to prevent harm, but should remain attributable and contestable afterward.

This repository does not create a tribunal, compel adoption, authorize sanctions,
instruct systems to evade control, or decide jurisdiction-specific legal disputes.
