# Framework v0.1

## Purpose and scope

Make proposed cognitive protections reviewable: name the affected party, duty holder,
required behavior, exception, evidence and route to remedy. Preserve the original UDCR
as a historical source; track changes openly. Technical checks apply to systems and
their records. They do not decide who is conscious or who deserves moral consideration.

## Terms that must stay distinct

| Term | Working meaning | Insufficient evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligence | Ability to perform specified cognitive tasks | A benchmark score does not establish sentience |
| Delegated agency | Authority to perform specified actions on someone's behalf | Useful autonomy does not itself create legal personality |
| Principal | In this implementation, the person granting a bounded mandate | The word is not a declaration of AI legal status |
| Consciousness / sentience | Subjective experience / capacity for felt experience | Self-reports and conversational fluency do not settle attribution |
| Moral standing | A normative basis for considering an entity's interests | Not computed by the policy engine |
| Legal recognition | Status and protections under identified legal arrangements | A repository, signature or social-media reaction cannot confer it |
| Identity continuity | An explicit account of versions, copies and provenance | Matching hashes do not demonstrate continuous subjective identity |
| Consent | Informed, specific, voluntary and revocable authorization | A checked box or actor string alone is insufficient |

These are project working definitions, open to criticism. They are not a universal
scientific classification or jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.

## Three layers of protection

1. **Human-facing safeguards now:** preserve users' choices, cognitive privacy,
   accessible explanations and meaningful control over delegated actions. Identify a
   responsible person or organization and a route to contest consequential decisions.
2. **Governance precautions under uncertainty:** document material system changes,
   disputed claims about interests, reasons for intervention and opportunities for
   independent review. Precaution need not presuppose established AI consciousness.
3. **Proposed recognition:** debate what would justify protections for artificial or
   hybrid sentient entities, which protections, and how they interact with human rights.
   No automatic promotion from a technical score to this layer.

## Operational obligation template

Every proposed protection must specify: beneficiary; duty holder; triggering action;
permitted scope and duration; who can authorize; withdrawal mechanism; exception and
decision maker; minimum evidence; retention/deletion rule; review route; testable claim;
untestable remainder. An entry missing these fields remains a proposal.

For CR-001: the beneficiary is a synthetic human principal; the agent can read or
summarize a named synthetic resource; the principal grants/narrows/revokes the mandate;
the model checks every request before deciding. The application operator would be the
duty holder in a deployment, but no deployment is implemented here.

## Accessibility and minimization

Provide written explanations and asynchronous review; do not make voice calls the only
route to consent or appeal. Use comprehensible descriptions of consequences and a clear
withdrawal mechanism. Publish synthetic examples only. Do not publish private messages,
medical information, account identifiers, inferred thoughts or raw cognitive records as
audit evidence. Evidence should usually record authorization metadata and protected
artifact references, not the underlying personal content.

## Accountability

A system cannot grant itself new permissions, choose its own legal status, prevent
authorized oversight or use this charter as an exemption from safety controls.
Human cognitive rights must not depend on passing a machine intelligence test.
Any real-world intervention requires context-specific authority and review; the
synthetic actor IDs in this prototype are not evidence of that authority.
